| Operate | **The seats and the arm** (`bin/seat/seats.sh` · `bin/agents/hall.sh` · `bin/pi/arm.sh`) | Where the work actually is, said by the backend itself: every pane in herdr (or tmux if herdr is silent), each in its own state — `working`, `idle`, `shell`, or **DEAD** for a bare shell inside a disposable `.yggdrasil/` worktree whose errand is gone — with `close` refusing any pane a live agent holds. `hall.sh` is the whole house in one read: the seat, the door count from the generated inventory, the pending wakes, and the last runes with their dates (`--runes N`). `arm.sh` is the honest half of supervision: the watch loop is harness-agnostic (`ymir-syn-watch.service`) and the session side is an extension, so it says **possible** on Pi and OpenCode (already installed), prints the `SessionStart` hook for Claude and Hermes, and names Codex and Cursor as unable — *named, never faked*. With no session bus it says `unknown`, never `absent` | [The seats and the arm](docs/features/the-seats-and-the-arm.md) |
